## Decisions made (and why)
- **No per-state time bands.** UP's own figures match the national cap. A state whose works close
  suspiciously fast (87 Bihar libraries "complete" a median 6 days after sanction) would make its
  own cap stricter for honest works.
- **One poor video is forgiven, two are a question.** Forgiving every poor video would let an agency
  film badly every month and pass every check.
- **The 60 m hall is a note, not a question.** 50 m is the rule's one-site line. A pin just past it
  is named for the officer, and the first site video settles it. The owner's "by map pin" rule is
  kept as decided.
- **The pin split keeps the strictest amounts** (every piece 96–100% of a limit). It only adds the
  pins as a second way to see that the pieces are one place.

## Problems, traps, surprises
- The sandbox cannot reach any government site (eSAKSHI, data.gov.in, UP PWD, CPWD); the web
  fetcher is blocked too. The team pack's dashboard tables are the region's real data.
- My first capture test replayed the same video file as visit 2, and the agent rightly called it
  reused footage. Each visit needs freshly drawn frames.
- A road subtype not in the adapter's list ("Gravel / WBM", "Other") became OTHER and lost its far
  end. That silently switched off every check by ends.
- Validation passed lines with a quantity but no rate as "ready".
