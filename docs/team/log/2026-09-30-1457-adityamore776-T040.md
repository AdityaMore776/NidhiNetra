## What I did
- `data/build_delay_model.py`: label = not completed within 365 days of recommendation. Features are
  facts known on the day of recommendation: the office's and district's past records, the office's
  backlog, type, size, month. LightGBM (not XGBoost: already installed, same kind of model). Its
  exact per-feature contributions (tree SHAP, `pred_contrib=True`) become each work's three reasons
  in plain words. Scores for open works go to `work_signals["delay"]`.
- `agents/delay/agent.py` + `engine.py`: reads the stored score as a RANK among open works. A finding
  only for the riskiest 10%. Wording never says "will be late". Completed works and works already
  past a year are NOT_APPLICABLE (the second is Compliance R02's job); no score = NOT_ASSESSABLE.
- Registered with `weight=0.0` + `weight_note` ("Shown, not counted: accuracy 0.71 against a 0.80 target").

## Numbers measured (only from saved script output)
- Strict backtest (train on works recommended Apr–Dec 2023, test on Jan–May 2025, 27,924 works):
  AUC **0.7095** vs the office's own record 0.6791. Riskiest 10%: 80.1% late (all test works 70.9%).
- Held-out districts (5 folds): AUC 0.588 (office record 0.419).
- 54,485 open works scored.

## Decisions made (and why)
- Misses the blueprint's 0.80 target → shown on each work but weight 0 in the risk score (AGENTS.md
  rule 4: no weight without a met target).

## Problems, traps, surprises
- Most MPLADS works miss the one-year norm (71% in the test period), so the score only means something
  as a rank among open works.
