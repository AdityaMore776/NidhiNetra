## Decisions made (and why)
- Cost uses the word-price model for every work although LightGBM caught more 5× mark-ups (55% vs ~43%): LightGBM
  can't be applied to a new work in the pure-Python engine, and two models for old/new works would be exactly
  the dataset-fitting the owner asked to remove. Word pairs + word-within-type were tried: no better (×1.84).
- Duplicate & Split dropped the meaning model (needs sentence-transformers, not in the engine) for lexical
  trigram similarity: measured equal or better on fresh samples, and it runs live.
- Delay stays weight 0 (0.705 < 0.80).
- Bands: HIGH stays where two engines must agree (0.22); CRITICAL moved to 0.30 because single signals now grade.
- 12 → 8 merge (owner asked "about 8"): see AGENT-CATALOG "Twelve checkers merged into eight".
