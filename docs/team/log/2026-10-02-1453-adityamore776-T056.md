  `apps/engine/drishti/agents/evidence.py` checks every part once (files, stamps, certificates, payments,
  fingerprints, photo readings, judgements) and drops what is malformed. Every engine and index reads through it.

  NOT_ASSESSABLE.

  zero inside numbers only (`reader.zeros_in_numbers`).

  Rejected a single-unit rule (it would change 1 of 653 real single-unit works). Read 12 random real Cost flags by
  eye (3 yes, 5 grey, 4 no) and fixed the main "no" cause: one amount for several streets is a bundle for the
  price (`scope.several_ways`), and only for the price (Duplicate & Split keeps a village's roads as one place).
