## What I did
The owner asked for other agent architectures to be tested against the current ones:
- test aggressively;
- write down what is better;
- switch only what is proven better and still immune to the loopholes the loop closed.

`data/trial/architecture_bakeoff.py` (new) runs each candidate on the same benchmark, its free
line set to the same honest-case rate. Results are in `results/architecture_bakeoff_{cost,site,dup}`.

- **Cost** (`cost`): six candidate whole-estimate decisions on 500 planted forms × 12 scenarios.
  - The scenarios: per-line spreads; carriage on bulk materials; a remote-site level on every
    line; a scarce district; noisy agencies; the SoR.
  - **Kept the current rule.**
    - z-tests read a remote site as padding: up to 2.8% of honest forms against 0% when lines
      move independently.
    - The isolation forest catches 34.5% of paddings against the current rule's 63.1%.
  - "Current OR a peer-adaptive line" asks no more honest forms and catches more only in tight
    districts (+15% padding at a 5% spread: 50.4% → 100%). It rests on that assumption, so it is
    **not switched**; the code is in the bake-off script.
- **Site Progress** (`site`): a simulated year of monthly visits, checked at every month's end,
  for 2,000 honest works and 500 of each of 8 tricks. Assumed error rates, tried at ×0.5, ×1 and
  ×1.5.
  - **Switched.**
    - "The last two videos unusable" became 4 of the last 5, or every video so far from the third.
    - "Filmed elsewhere" needs the GPS 300 m+ out; nearer, the views count as unusable.
    - Honest works asked a year: 25.7% → 5.0% (×1), 10.4% → 2.9% (×0.5), 45.0% → 9.4% (×1.5).
      Tricks are still caught 79–100%.
  - Two loopholes found and closed: three bad videos and then nothing, and `COMPLETE_NOT_FILMED`
    (recorded complete 30+ days ago with no final video).
  - Rejected: a CUSUM running score (37–39% of on-purpose bad videos caught).
  - Kept: the claim rule (owner's design); T126 decides it.
- **Duplicate Work** (`dup`): **switched** to a map grid (550 m cells, looked up within 200 m of
  the pins). The answers are the same and it is 8–10x faster: a 10,000-form district's batch goes
  from 726 s to 91 s.
- **Fusion:** not re-tested. Its labelled planted benchmark needs the research build, and the new
  questions all sit at the prior.

## Decisions made (and why)
- **A benchmark resting on an assumption must survive the assumption moving.**
  - Site Progress was tried at ×0.5 to ×1.5 errors, and the switch wins at every level.
  - Cost's peer line wins only if tight districts exist. Not switched.
- **Correlated honest variation goes in the Cost benchmark.** A remote site lifts every line. Tests
  that assume independent lines look great without it and fail with it.
- **The claim rule stays** as the owner set it ("twice in a row"). "3 of the last 5" trades 5–12
  points of ghost catches for fewer honest questions; the reader's real error rate (T126) decides.

## Problems, traps, surprises
- My own T136 rule ("the last two unusable") would have asked about 1 honest work in 4 a year.
  The rule tests passed; only a simulation checked every month showed it.
- "Filmed elsewhere" fired on GPS jitter plus lost views in the same video, for 15 of 2,000
  honest works a year.
- Rate statistics that assume independent lines are fragile; the money gap is not.
