## What I did
The owner asked for a simpler demonstration: show what each agent flags in the form and why, short; and since there
is no real price data yet, a separate Data area showing the example data in basic words.
- **Page** `/work-form`: each agent now says Flagged / Clear / Not checked / After sanction with one line on why,
  built from the finding's own numbers (`src/lib/work-form-flags.ts`); the flagged fields are outlined inside the
  form in the agent's colour with a short label ("needs 1,334", "usual ₹380", "20 m from WF-OLD-ROAD"); "Show in
  form" scrolls to the field. Long engine sentences and stories are gone from the panel.
- **Data tab** (`src/components/nn/work-form-data.tsx`): the prices other agencies filed, the standard solar-light
  price (real eSAKSHI works), the works on record, what happened after sanction (dates and site videos), and the
  rules the agents use.
- **Live check uses the same example data**: `POST /work-form/check?demo=true` adds `drishti/demo.py`'s records
  (`reference/demo/work_form.json`, written by the examples script) placed in the form's district, and gives an
  example filing its record after sanction. Nothing stored; no other route reads it. So a judge can change a field
  and see the flag go away.
- **Engine** (additive): explain findings carry `facts` (a whitelisted part of the evidence: numbers, dates, work IDs,
  item names); Cost's whole-estimate evidence names its top lines; Site Progress evidence carries the distance and the
  claims; `batch.with_index(..., extra=)`.
- **Examples script**: one-line stories; writes the Data block and the engine's demo file; refuses to write unless
  the demo path raises exactly each example's flags.

## Decisions made (and why)
- The demo data is applied only when the page asks (`demo=true`), so no real check ever compares with made-up
  filings, and the Data tab shows exactly what was used (both are written by one script).
- Example records are placed in whichever district the form names, so a judge's own form in another district is
  still compared with the example prices.
- A field flagged twice shows one label (the first flag's), to keep the form readable.

## Problems, traps, surprises
- The engine names estimate lines by its catalogue ("Cement", "Unskilled labour"), not the agency's words, so the
  page finds a line's form row by its quantity and rate.
- `locator("main")` matches two elements on this layout (the sidebar inset is a `main` too); use `.last`.
