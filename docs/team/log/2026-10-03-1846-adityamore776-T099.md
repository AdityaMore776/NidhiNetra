## Decisions made (and why)
- **No price question from a record:** 0 of 15 were real.
- **No counted repeat from words:** 2 in 3 at best. The form's map pin and the agency's own answers make it certain.
- **Kept the strictest split.** It reads 12 of 14 yes; dropping it would have lost nine real splits that were in the
  queue.
- **New findings count at the 1-in-4 prior** until officers' verdicts are read (T098). The split is measured (12 of
  14).
