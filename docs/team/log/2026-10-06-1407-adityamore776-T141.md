## Decisions made (and why)
- **Labour within 2× of its trade is settled in the whole estimate and kept out of the share's
  base.** Counting wages there asked about honest crews (the owner ruled that out). Keeping them
  in the base would let a large honest crew, never judged, dilute padded materials. The cost: more
  honest questions at a 15% material spread; the owner sets the margins on real forms (T098).
- **A held typo line counts toward coverage, at its own value.** Counting it as unreadable money
  turned an honest typo into a question.
- **Labour among the BOQ lines is a confirm, not a refusal.** "JCB with operator" would be blocked
  wrongly; the rate is judged by trade either way.
- **The padded demo road pads 25%, not 22%.** At 22% of materials only, moving the padding into
  "other charges" falls under the 15% line, so the probes would test a thinner padding than before.
