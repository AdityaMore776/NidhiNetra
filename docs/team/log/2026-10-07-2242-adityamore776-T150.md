## Decisions made (and why)
- **Owner, 7 Oct 2026:**
  - **Headline (Track A):** not fooled by staged sites, light or camera angle, at about ₹0 a check. **Track B next:** the same accuracy at 1/100 of the cost.
  - **Compute:** free only (RTX 3070 Ti 8 GB + Kaggle).
  - **Sources:**
    - government stage photos;
    - **YouTube CC BY** (this answers T147's question for CC BY; NC and ND stay refused);
    - open datasets, Wikimedia, Kaggle and GitHub.

    The team's own monthly films were not chosen this round.
  - **Deliverable:** a repo doc plus a picker page.
- **The VLM complements T146; it does not replace it.** T146's pipeline is the data engine and the "where" map. The VLM is the claim judge for check 6, and replaces the Gemini dependency.
- **Base model:** Qwen3-VL-4B (2B for Track B). This follows ProgressLM: a 3B model with SFT on answer-given reasons, then GRPO, beat GPT-5 at progress estimation.
- **Rewards:** being fooled (calling a staged or light-only pair "shown") costs −1.5. "Can't tell" on an answerable item costs −0.2, so abstaining never becomes a hiding place.
- **The test set:** human labels only, from districts never used in training, never online before the final run.
- **Rule 8:** the flagship models are benchmark baselines, not rival teams. The deck still shows the comparison only on the References slide (said in the doc).
