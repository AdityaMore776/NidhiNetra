## Decisions made (and why)
- Grey out the writing instead of answering "can't tell" when there is writing: 229 of 300 real photos have some, and
  finished MPLADS works are meant to carry a plaque, so refusing would cost honest works their confirmation.
- The claim guard refuses only instruction words; a claim that misleads in plain words is read like any claim.
