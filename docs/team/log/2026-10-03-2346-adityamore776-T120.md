## Decisions made (and why)
- **`SANCTIONED_LATE` is a fact and goes on the office report card; it never queues a work alone.** 60% of works break
  the clock, so as a flag it would fill the queue.
- **The comfort window uses each work's own estimate, checked against a band.** One window per type would be wrong
  (the spread is 8× to 367×).
  - A fast finish counts only when the videos do not show the stages, so good work is not punished.
  - A padded estimate buys no time: the window caps at the usual longest time for the kind and size.
- **Site Progress places people on the site by matching what the camera sees at fixed viewpoints, not by exact
  GPS.** Phone GPS is about ±5 m, and phone AR drifts by metres. GPS answers "is this the site?", and a place check
  needs GPS and the views to agree.
- **Cost judges the whole estimate** (15%+ and Rs 50,000+ above the fair estimate, with 60%+ of the money compared).
  It also fact-checks lines that cite an SoR code. These thresholds are starting values that §14 measures.
