## What I did
- Plan: `docs/plans/T030-dup-split.md`.
- Data job `data/build_dup_split.py`: meaning vectors (paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2, local,
  free; cached in data/cache) for 207,881 readable descriptions, compared within each district.
  Duplicate pairs (text + place + time + amount score); split groups; results to
  `data/build/dup_split.parquet` → `work_signals["dup_split"]` (migration 006, from T020).
- Engine `apps/engine/drishti/agents/dup_split/`: reads the stored links. Funded again after
  completion 0.85, repeated while open 0.60, same-site split 0.60, bulk order 0.35, +0.20 when every
  piece is Rs 4–5 lakh with a total over Rs 5 lakh. Each group member carries the group id (fusion
  counts a group once). Not run / no description, date or amount → NOT_ASSESSABLE.
- Four fixes, each found by READING the output, each followed by a fresh sample:
  1. Chains of an MP's alike works for different villages were one "group" (31% of works in
     groups, and the Nanded split lost inside one) → groups cut by place words.
  2. Different survey / property numbers or road stretches matched → different numbers = different site.
  3. Wording one MP filed 3+ times (7 identical water coolers) → a series, not a duplicate pair.
  4. `token_set_ratio` scored a subset as 100 (shared panchayat name = "same place") →
     whole place lists must match (`token_sort_ratio`).
- By-eye labels `data/trial/dup_split_labels.py`, scorer `data/trial/dup_split_eye.py`.
- FINDINGS: new "Duplicate & Split engine" section under A2.
