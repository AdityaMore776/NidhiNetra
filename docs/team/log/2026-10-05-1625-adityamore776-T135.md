## Decisions made (and why)
- **Off, never guessed.** A price the data cannot support is switched off for that material and
  recorded as a gap. A state-wide rate gets 10% more leniency, so it never asks where the
  district's own rate would not.
- **Missing data versus evasion.** Missing price data is our gap, so it is never a finding. An
  estimate the agency's own form makes uncheckable (lump sums, unnamed items, a contradicted size)
  is a question.
- **The gap's measure.** It is measured against the fair price of what was priced. This keeps the
  measured target at a 10% spread and catches more (94.2%).
- **Two proofs stay.** "Filmed elsewhere" still needs both GPS and views. A pin that every video
  disagrees with the same way is a new, separate question.
- **The six LIMITs stay**, with their reasons in the plan. Each needs data we do not have yet (the
  SoR, real forms, size measured on video), or is a measured threshold.

## Problems, traps, surprises
- The website-form adapter never let a filing with a lump sum be judged (no source on the line).
- My first `run()` helper dropped the replacement copies with the originals: a probe bug, not an
  agent bug. When a probe fails, check the probe first.
- Changing rates in a probe changes the total and the gap to the MP's figure. The form then rightly
  asks for a reason, so move the MP's figure too.
- The rate index never looks ahead: only forms filed before the checked one count.
