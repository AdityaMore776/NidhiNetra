## Decisions made (and why)
- Candidates = the queue's own rule (band CRITICAL/HIGH, one stop per case), not the blueprint's "risk >= 40": the
  calibrated bands put HIGH at 50 and the planner must match what the queue page shows.
- No OR-Tools: a day holds at most ~12 places, where Held-Karp is exact and instant (0.03 s at 12); avoids a big
  dependency on the Vercel function (owner agreed).
- Spot checks only SANCTIONED or COMPLETED works (something to see at the site); a district with no queue cases gets
  an empty plan, not a spot-check-only day.
- **The planner is public** (shown in the public view's nav): it is the headline and the owner chose real works.
  It is the only public page that lists real works rated from their records; descriptions are masked by `pii.mask`.
  To make it staff-only: add `signedIn: true` to its item in `apps/web/src/components/shell/nav.ts`.

## Problems, traps, surprises
- The India Post directory gives many branch offices their parent's exact position (34,459 offices on positions
  shared 3+ ways): such a match is labelled "area", never "village".
- Office names are one word where descriptions split them ("Rachandar Thirumalai" = office "rachandarthirumalai"):
  the matcher joins 1-4 word runs before looking up.
- A district's name in another spelling ("Nellore" for "Sri Potti Sriramulu Nellore") is boilerplate, not a place.
- The public view answers /dashboard/districts from the examples file (lib/showcase.ts), so the planner needed its
  own `/plans/districts`.
- A Playwright scroll in 120 ms steps did not trigger the Reveal fade-in (looked like a blank section); at normal
  speed it shows. Not a bug.
