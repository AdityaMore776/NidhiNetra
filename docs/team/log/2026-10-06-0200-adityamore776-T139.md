**Round 1** (`data/trial/real_geo_check.py`): Jaunpur's 1,038 real works (dates, amounts, MPs,
kinds), each pinned at a real building near one of the district's 106 post offices, with phone
GPS error. Planted repeats file a sanctioned work again 6–18 months later at the same building.
- Houses stand 8.3 m apart (median).
- 1,006 of the works are solar street lights at named houses. Under the 50 m rule, 37–87% of
  honest light works were asked "already sanctioned here?".
- Half of one institution's honest purchases were asked (computers, then furniture).

**Round 2:** each fix measured on its own (the results show every variant).
1. A light filed the same day as another, in one programme, is never a repeat of it.
2. A light is one spot: 20 m, not 50 m. At 10 m too many real repeats were lost.
3. Purchases are compared by work type.

Honest lights asked now: 1.5–6.4%. Honest purchases asked: 2.8%. Repeats asked: 93–100%.

**Round 3:** writing the loop's probes showed my first same-day rule (for every kind of work)
let a hall filed twice on one day pass. I narrowed it to lights, and that probe now passes.

**Site Progress:** all 1,025 real completion timelines were run with honest videos, and nothing
was asked. That showed the loophole:
- 532 of the works were closed within the month of sanction;
- with no first video, "already built at the start" can never be asked, so an old asset filmed
  once as the final video passes as new.

New question `FIRST_FILMED_FINISHED` (at the prior until read). Progress filmed in between, or a
declared upgrade, is not asked.

**Time & Rules:** 9,830 of the 40,935 works were asked. 9,401 of those are the 75-day clock fact

## Decisions made (and why)
- **Buildings keep the 50 m line and the family.**
  - 12–15% of honest building works were asked, but only with buildings placed at random large
    buildings. Open Buildings counts house clusters as large, so that is an upper bound.
  - The family also stops a hall re-filed as a "library" from passing.
- **20 m for lights, not 10 or 15 m.** Two GPS fixes of one house differ by more than the gap to
  the next house. 15 m kept 84–97% of repeats; 20 m keeps 93–98%. A light filed again a little
  off still has its first video showing an old light (Site Progress).
- **The same-day exception is for lights only.** A programme of lights is many houses; two halls
  at one pin on one day are one hall.
