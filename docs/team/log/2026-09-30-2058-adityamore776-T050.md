## Decisions made (and why)
- Cut-offs tuned on the data, as T050 asks. The blueprint's 40/70/85 give zero works above Low (max 37), and
  its 5–10% High + Critical target has no cut-off: scores are stepped, 2.3% → 17%. Chosen: High = two engines
  each with a meaningful signal; Critical = rule issue + split under Rs 5 lakh / repeat after completion, or
  three engines. They are three constants in `fuse.py` if the owner wants a bigger or smaller queue.
- Review queue = High + Critical (blueprint: alerts for High/Critical). Medium works stay searchable.

## Problems, traps, surprises
