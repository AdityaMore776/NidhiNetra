## What I did
- Owner's complaint: odd prices (library building vs book orders = "hundreds of times typical", many
  works at an identical "5.0x", every queued work "Medium").
- `agents/statistical/scope.py` (new): reads what a description covers. Undescribed text becomes
  NOT_ASSESSABLE. Many-site lists, bundles and counts ("10 nag", "110 pc", "4 adad") are read as
  several jobs. Per-unit and per-metre readings where a count or length is named.
- `agent.py` / `cohort.py` / `data/build_stats.py`: scope-aware peers (LIBRARY and STREET_LIGHT split
  into GOODS/WORKS, high masts their own group). A work is flagged only above 2× the p90 of its
  district peers AND above the state/national top. Risk is graded (`above_risk`) instead of one flat
  value. More than 10× the median is worded as a scope question (`SCOPE_FAR_LARGER`), not a price.
- Model B `data/build_cost_model.py`: LightGBM quantiles on description words, out-of-fold by
  constituency. The engine flags only when the peers and the model agree.
- Generic offline-signal table: migration `006_work_signals.sql`; `batch.py` SIGNALS join;
  `data/common.py` SIGNALS; `data/load_db.py load_signals`. The delay and duplicate engines use it too.
- `registry.py`: an engine may set `weight=0.0` ("shown, not counted") plus a `weight_note`.
- `validation/pii.py`: masks phone numbers in descriptions returned by the API.
- API test fixture: the "costly" work lowered from Rs 20 lakh to Rs 10 lakh. At 12× the median the
  new rules correctly call it a scope question, so it no longer tested the price path.
