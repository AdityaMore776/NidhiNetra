## Decisions made (and why)
- Count only the MEDIUM reads. Applying the measured rate to the LOW records notes would have lifted 1,084 works
  that the design treats as one note per office (the reason bulk entry and office habits are LOW), so their weight is
  pinned where it was.
- "Yes" on the R13/R14 standard (the dates need checking), not "proven wrong": a work recorded complete before it was
  recommended is either a wrong date or a work funded after it was built; both are for the officer to check.
- Kerala (8 of 10) is noted, not acted on: one office's habit is already caught per office; a state-wide habit would
  need a state rate the engine does not compute. If more reads show it, treat the state's cases like bulk entry.

## Problems, traps, surprises
- The masker (`validation/pii.py`) missed two kinds of private names in the printed descriptions: "<name> house"
  (road from one person's house to another's, WS/MP678/2023-2024/37188) and "<name> land" (WS/MP310/2023-2024/12784).
  It also over-masks "10 Mr" (10 metre) as an honorific. Not fixed here: "<x> house" and "<x> land" also cover pump
  houses, guest houses and government land, so a rule needs its own measurement on real descriptions.
- R06's stored risk is the severity signal (MEDIUM 0.6, LOW 0.3), not the rule's own 0.10-0.30: one hit rate per code
  means a measured rate lifts every severity of that code unless the weaker ones carry their own risk.
