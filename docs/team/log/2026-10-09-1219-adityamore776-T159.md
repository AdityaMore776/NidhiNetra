## Decisions made (and why)
- **The filmed spot is compared only for buildings and places**, and needs most videos to agree and each
  to be nearer the earlier building than the form's pin, so an honest new hall 75 m from an old one,
  filmed around its own pin, is never asked (a test).
- **OTHER work type left open**: comparing OTHER works with buildings at one pin could ask honest pairs (a
  water tank beside a school); it waits for real forms.
- **Public repository = a separate repository**, rebuilt by the script, suggested name
  `AdityaMore776/NidhiNetra-SIH` (it matches https://nidhinetra-sih.vercel.app). A new name also means no
  old clone can push this repository's history into it by mistake.
- **Honest statements stay** in the public copy: "the labels were checked by an AI model" (NidhiLens
  report) and `data/trial/dashboard_labels.py` ("Read by an AI model"): without them the accuracy figures
  would read as checked by people.
