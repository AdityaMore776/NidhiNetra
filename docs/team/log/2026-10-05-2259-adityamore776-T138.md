## Decisions made (and why)
- The agent has no per-line price limit (it judges the whole estimate), so the column shows the band
  other agencies charge as "normal" and the real comfort limit (whole estimate +15%) underneath. It
  does not invent a per-line limit.
- "Could it be justified?" items are read from the form. The agent does not discount for them, so a
  yes says "the officer weighs it", never "valid".
