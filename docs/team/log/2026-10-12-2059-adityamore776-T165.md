## What I did
- Owner said the buttons that switch what a page shows ("Data the agents use", "Section by section") were hard to see.
  They were grey text on a grey track.
- `Segmented` (the one shared switch) now has a light-blue track with a blue border, a filled brand-blue pill with white
  text on the chosen option, dark-blue text on the others, and an optional "VIEW" label, icons and a small size.
- The three hand-made grey switches now use `Segmented`: /work-form ("Form and flags" / "Data the agents use"),
  /end-to-end and the example form ("Section by section" / "The form as filed"). The queue band filter and the
  planner's hours and language switches pick up the new look automatically.
- New `.more-toggle` class in `globals.css` for buttons that open extra detail: tinted pill with an arrow that flips
  when open. Used on "Show blank fields too", "N answers missing for Cost" and "The N other agencies' road filings".
- The arrow on each form section (A, B, C...) in the section-by-section view now sits in a small blue badge.
