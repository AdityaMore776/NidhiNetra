## What I did
The owner asked for:
- a data ingestion pipeline of construction site videos;
- a tuned dataset with specific augmentation;
- an existing vision model fine-tuned until it is "very good at identifying actual construction progress".

The owner's notes were ideas to judge, not instructions.
- **Judged every note** in `docs/plans/T146-site-progress-vision.md` §2:
  - **Kept, changed:** "what is temporary" (as irreversibility, not speed); "walk back from the final scene" (as
    hindsight: "never went back", not "looks like the end", because covered work never looks like the end); splitting
    by site (tightened: re-uploads and channels too); removing people (masked, never repainted); text labels (as a
    stage list, marked where each stage begins).
  - **The overfitting worry:** kept, but measured by the train-vs-test gap and by leave-one-type-out, not by
    parameter count.
  - **Rejected as the judge:** human activity and tool use. Activity is not progress and is the easiest thing to stage
    on filming day (T120 §9.6). Activity is kept as metadata only.
  - **Added:** ordering pretraining ("Made to Order", ECCV 2024), synthetic pairs ("The Change You Want to See", WACV
    2023), a frozen DINOv2 + small head, shortcut audits.
- **Built `apps/vision/`** (`python -m drishti_vision check | search | fetch | build | synth | train | measure`):
  - **getting videos:** a manifest with a licence allow-list; polite fetching with checksums;
  - **cleaning frames:** shots, alignment, date-stamp and moving-thing masks;
  - **labels and data:** the hindsight teacher; pairs; splits by site and channel; augmentation; the dataset writer and
    its card;
  - **the model:** a PyTorch two-frame model on a DINOv2 backbone, with every plan §8 measure;
  - **testing aids:** a synthetic-timelapse generator with exact truth; a Kaggle runner for the GPU.

## Numbers measured (only from saved script output)
- **Teacher vs truth:** patch precision 0.913, recall 0.957, IoU 0.877. Pairs labelled 96.1%, right 97.5% (93.9%
  without stage marks). 16.9% of buried patches called progress.
- **Masks:** date stamp IoU 0.785 (lowest 0.633); 97.4% of people patches masked; 100% of frames aligned. The re-upload
  joined its site; 0 leaks.
- **Student vs truth on unseen sites:**
  - in-type: AUROC 0.960, train−test gap +0.018;
  - light called progress 0.6%, a dressed-up site 7.0%, both directions 0.3%;
  - without augmentation: AUROC 0.968, but 10.1%, 21.8% and 7.5%;
  - held-out kind: AUROC 0.918 (road) to 0.976 (masonry);
  - build-up τ 0.02–0.34 (target 0.8, missed).

## Decisions made (and why)
- **Irreversibility, not speed, and not "looks like the final":** see the plan §2. Speed fails on fast work (a slab in
  a day) and slow temporaries (scaffolds for weeks); "like the final" fails on covered work.
- **Thresholds from each shot's own noise:** the same rule works for hand-made and DINOv2 features and any camera.
  It comes within 0.02 IoU of the best fixed value.
- **Frozen backbone + small head (1.9 M weights on DINOv2-B):** the user's overfitting worry is answered by measuring
  (gap, leave-one-type-out), and the size is chosen on held-out sites among a probe (0.34 M), the head, and head + LoRA.
- **YouTube downloads off by default:** the CC licence is not YouTube's terms. NC and ND licences are refused too.
  Both wait for the owner.
- **Put on the open PR's branch** (claude/hopeful-feynman-4ov8y0), as this session's designated branch.

## Problems, traps, surprises
- **This machine's network refuses YouTube, Wikimedia, Hugging Face, Kaggle, arxiv and download.pytorch.org.** Only
  PyPI, npm and GitHub get through. PyTorch came from PyPI with its CUDA libraries (about 6 GB) to run on CPU.
- **Fixes along the way** (FINDINGS "T146"):
  - alignment: keypoints spread over a grid, and a similarity fit;
  - a shadow taken for a date stamp, and for moving things;
  - hand-made features too alike for the head to learn;
  - rare progress patches needing weight.
- **A synthetic toy test can lie:** adding a constant to every feature dimension is invisible after LayerNorm. Test
  with a random direction.
- Python's stdout is buffered through a pipe: run long scripts with `python -u`.
