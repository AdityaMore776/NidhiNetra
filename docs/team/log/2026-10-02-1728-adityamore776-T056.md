## Problems, traps, surprises
- **Stored findings exist only for HIGH and CRITICAL works.** Any SQL count of a finding over `assessments.agents`
  undercounts everything that is MEDIUM or LOW (R06, R01/R02 waits, office habits, most Cost flags). Count from the
  works table and re-run the rule, or re-assess in memory.
- A saved trial result can predate a later engine change: compare a re-run with the code's history before blaming
  the newest change.
