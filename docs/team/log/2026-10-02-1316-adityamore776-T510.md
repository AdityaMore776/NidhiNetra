## Decisions made (and why)
- Readers run offline and store readings, not answers (T051 rule; a model is too heavy for the live API).
- "Not shown" needs 2+ usable photos, none showing the asset, no progress photos, and a group where absence
  can be seen (roads, buildings, walls, ponds, dams, drains, crossings, shelters; never lights, pumps, goods).
- No plaque in the photos is a fact, never a finding (a photo may not frame it).
- Off by default, then weight 0 even after GO, until officer verdicts give it a hit rate (fusion v3).
- The v1 satellite gate is not carried over; B5 keeps its own go/no-go (T604).

## Problems, traps, surprises
- RapidOCR drops spaces between words: patterns over its text must not rely on word boundaries.
- RapidOCR's bundled models read Latin script only: Hindi plaques need another OCR or the Gemini reader.
- `data/common.py` imports the database driver, so the offline job (trial venv) has its own small `clean()`.
