## What I did
- **`agents/cost_estimate/`** (new engine, shown, not counted, weight 0):
  - `reference.py` builds price-per-unit groups from a state's real works; `data/build_cost_reference.py` writes
    `reference/cost/uttar_pradesh.json`.
  - `agent.py` reads the agency's form (`work["cost_form"]`) and gives two kinds of question:
    - `MATERIAL_QUANTITY_ABOVE_SIZE`: a confirmed material more than 25% above what the size needs.
    - `ESTIMATE_ABOVE_STANDARD_AND_MP`: the price per unit more than 25% above the 98th percentile of the same
      standard item in the district or state, **and** the estimate more than 10% above the MP's amount.
  - Everything else becomes a note or "could not check".

  - light fittings, batteries, panels and poles per solar light;

  The form (validate.py) now asks the agency to confirm a quantity above that.
- **Rules forced by the data** (plan §11):
  - A group is compared only when it is tight (p90 within +25% of the median) **and** priced in the form's own unit
    of size. UP records never state a road's width (0 of 1,129), so roads are never compared per metre.
  - A price question needs two signals, because records hold no wattage or height.

## Decisions made (and why)
- **Two signals for a price question:** the price above the standard item by more than 25% (beyond p98) **and** above
  the MP's amount by more than 10%. A bigger item, or an MP's low guess, can each explain one signal alone.
- **The unit-of-size rule:** a tight group of roads per metre (Deoria) would still flag a wide road against narrow
  ones, so a group must be priced in the form's own unit.
- **Quantity questions only on materials the agency confirmed,** and only beyond +25%. The form already asked them to
  confirm anything above the size's need, so a typo cannot reach the officer.
- **The old cost card's hand-off moves to T098.** The new engine is weight 0, so nothing is counted twice meanwhile.

## Problems, traps, surprises
- **"Tight" is not enough.** A district's roads can be homogeneous and still hide width. The unit has to carry the
  size.
- **A record of 215 masts under one price was really several sites:** the form's own limit refused it. That is what
  the form is for.
- **My first test suite passed at once, so I printed the real answers** to be sure the tests weren't passing for the
  wrong reason. The texts needed plain words for the specification ("LED 12 W", not "led_w 12").
