## Decisions made (and why)
- **Room before asking, never a guess at need:** each need is the size times a published
  proportion, with room for honest practice. Only a confirmed quantity 25% beyond it is asked,
  the margin every other quantity question uses.
- **House connections are the agency's call.** Their length depends on how far houses stand from
  the main. When they are on lines of their own, only the main is held to the pipeline's length.
- **The itemising rule is a fix, not a question.** A road's estimate that lists its base on its own
  line is what every schedule of rates expects; it costs an honest agency one line.
- **The SoR on branch `adityamore776/T096-up-sor` was not switched on.** It waits for a check by
  eye, per-circle rates and GST (its log), and the owner's go-ahead.
