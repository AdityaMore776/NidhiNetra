## What I did
- From an Indian connection, the .gov.in sites open (uppwd.gov.in, cpwd.gov.in, uplabour.gov.in,
  gem.gov.in all answered 200). UP PWD publishes its **Schedule of Rates 2025-26 per circle** at
  https://uppwd.gov.in/pages/en-leftmenu/schedule-of-rates (about 40 circles, each a main book and
  a later circle revision).
- Downloaded the **Jaunpur-Ghazipur circle** book (36 pages, from 01.05.2025) and its revision of
  15.10.2025 (2 pages) into `data/raw/rates/up_pwd/` (gitignored). Both are scans with no text layer.
- Read the pages and typed 38 rates into
  `apps/engine/drishti/reference/cost/sor_source/up_pwd_jaunpur_ghazipur_2025-26.csv`, one row per
  printed rate, each with its PDF page, the printed page, its basis and its effective date.
  Rows: materials (p.8-9), labour (p.10), Mirzapur quarry rates (p.5), and the 15.10.2025 revision.
- `data/build_sor.py` checks the table (known materials and units, a page on every row, a
  superseded or at-quarry rate is never mapped, one row per material) and writes the engine's
  SoR format to `reference/cost/sor_candidates/` (not `reference/cost/sor/`, which the engine
  reads).
- Mapped: cement Rs 300 a bag (small work, revision 15.10.2025), bricks Class 150 Rs 6,900 per
  1,000, unskilled labour Rs 423 a day, skilled labour Rs 521 a day.
- Not mapped, on purpose:
  - steel and large-work cement: the revision says "as per CPWD DSR", with no figure;
  - stone aggregate and coarse sand: the book gives prices at the Mirzapur quarry, without carriage
    and profit;
  - the local river sand: a different grade from concrete's coarse sand.
- Tests: `tests/agents/cost_estimate/test_sor_candidates.py` (5).

## Decisions made (and why)
- **Not switched on.** It waits for three things:
  1. The engine reads one SoR per **state**, but UP PWD sets rates per **circle**. A Jaunpur rate
     applied in Lucknow would be wrong, so the engine needs a per-district lookup first.
  2. Every rate in the book **excludes GST** (terms, item 1). Agencies' forms mostly quote rates
     with tax. Compared as they are, honest lines would look 5-28% high, so GST must be added before
     comparing.
  3. The owner's go-ahead (T098).
- Worth knowing: the book's cement (Rs 300 + GST) is close to the demo filings' Rs 380 with tax;
  its labour (Rs 423 / 521) is near the demo's Rs 420 unskilled, and below its Rs 750 skilled
  mason. The SoR's "Labour (skilled)" is a general skilled hand, while a mason grade-I is Rs 525.

## Problems, traps, surprises
- The scans have no text layer: OCR would be needed to do all 40 circles. The page images were
  read by eye here (pymupdf renders them; `pip install pymupdf pypdf` into the engine venv; not
  added to the engine's requirements because only this data script uses them).
- The book has a further revision (memo 3819, 01.07.2025) that is not on the website.
- **CPWD DSR 2023: no official download found.** cpwd.gov.in's publications page lists DSR up to
  2014 (English) and a 2022 Hindi version; the 2023 books turn up only on third-party sites
  (Scribd, SlideShare). These were not used: an official copy is needed (ask CPWD, or a teammate
  with an e-tender login).
- The `cpwd.gov.in` publication page loops on redirects unless curl keeps cookies (`-c/-b jar`).
