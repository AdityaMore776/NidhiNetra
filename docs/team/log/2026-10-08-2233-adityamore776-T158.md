## What I did

The owner asked (8 Oct): "mark where the user is filming from; next time tell them to be in that box and show the
same areas; if a project is too big for one spot, from its size, ask for more angles, and judge accordingly". The
design was agreed with the owner, with these choices:
- warn, never block;
- GPS box plus compass plus the faint picture (no OpenCV.js, no native app).

The plan is `docs/plans/T158-same-spot-filming.md`; its Status header lists what changed while building.

- **Engine:**
  - `sitemap.spots_needed`: a pair of spots per 40 m of the longest side, 2 to 8; a linear work films every 50 m
    stretch up to 1 km.
  - `sitemap.stretches_asked`: over 1 km, 6 stretches drawn from `capture.seed(visit_id)` (an HMAC only the server
    can make) plus both ends.
  - The site circle now uses the footprint: a pond's `water_area_m2` (it was ignored, so every pond got 25 m), and
    a building's area over its floors.
- **New `agents/site_progress/spots.py`:**
  - `spot`: the GPS median during the viewpoint, else the last good fix up to 60 s before;
  - `arc`: the compass arc, wrapping past north;
  - `covered`;
  - `anchors`: the first good fix, never creeping, with the facing kept separately;
  - `whole_claim`;
  - `combine`.
- **`video/visit.py`:**
  - Each viewpoint keeps its spot, its facing and 3 pan pictures, and a linear work keeps a picture per stretch.
  - It reads `spot_offsets_m`, `spot_coverage`, `spot_verdicts`, `spots_needed`/`spots_filmed`,
    `claim_spots_not_shown` and `stretches_missed`.
  - The claim is judged spot by spot (`judge([claim], [before-X, now-X])`), up to 12 spots.
  - `job.py` stores all of it.
- **API:**
  - Viewpoints carry `anchor`, `facing` and `pan_urls`.
  - GET and start say `spots_needed`; start returns `stretches_asked`.
  - The log limit is now 1 MB, to hold the compass.
- **Agent 3.4.0:**
  - CLAIM_NOT_SHOWN names the spots.
  - Notes, never findings: too few spots, a viewpoint more than 20 m from its spot, a pan under 70%, stretches not
    filmed.
  - The work page gets each visit's spots.
- **Phone:**
  - `SpotGuide.tsx`: a radar to the box, a facing dial with last time's arc, and last time's 3 pictures.
  - `capture.ts` logs the compass: the W3C camera heading, or `webkitCompassHeading` on iPhone.
  - The first visit is told the spots its size needs.
  - A long road's stretches are checked off as they are walked.
- **Work page:** `SiteSpots.tsx` adds a "the claim, spot by spot" line on the Site Progress card.

## Decisions made (and why)

- **The combining rule changed from the draft.**
  - The reason: a spot that does not face the part a claim names answers not_shown too. The VLM reader of T156
    already protects against this.
  - Now only a whole-work claim ("all", "every", "entire", "पूरा", "सभी"...) is not_shown on one spot's
    contradiction. A one-part claim needs every filmed spot to agree and at least half the needed spots filmed.
  - The owner approved the first draft; this change is safer for honest agencies, and is to be told to the owner.
- **The spot window is the viewpoint ±0.5 s**, not ±2 s: the walk up to the spot is not the spot.
- **The anchor is the first trusted good fix (≤15 m) and never moves after that.** This stops the box creeping
  month by month. The arc comes from the same visit, so it cannot shrink.
- **No migration:** `kept` and `log` are JSON already.

## Problems, traps, surprises

- **The AGENTS.md heredoc trap hit once, in a test** (`b"\xff\xd8"` became raw bytes). It was caught by a
  SyntaxError, and fixed with the editor. Edits now go through scratch scripts or the Edit tool.
- **Test data, twice:** a road of 0.0054° is 600.45 m (13 stretches, not 12), and 0.0018° is 200.15 m (5
  stretches, not 4).
- **The real-browser run found two bugs that unit tests could not:**
  - A phone standing still sends no new GPS fix, so only v1 got a box.
  - The facing hung on the GPS.

  Both are fixed and tested.
- **The replay found one more:** no compass read as "0% panned" at every spot.
- **`data/trial/capture_e2e.py` was Linux-only** (`os.killpg`, `npx`). It now runs on Windows too, using `npx.cmd`,
  `taskkill`, and Playwright's own Chromium in %LOCALAPPDATA%.
