## Decisions made (and why)
- A queue case with no row is "Not read yet"; its row and the 'open' entry are written at the first move. No batch
  step is needed, and nothing to run after each re-assessment.
- No foreign key to `works`: a reload empties works, and a case's history must survive it.
- One writer at a time (`pg_advisory_xact_lock`) keeps one chain; the client sends the state it saw, so two officers
  can never both move a case (the second gets 409).
- Added "Read by the office → Closed without a visit" (note required) so an office can drop a case that needs no
  visit; the digest names every such close of the week, so it cannot hide quietly.
- Ministry may read and close but not plan visits; the visit and what was found are recorded by the field officer
  or the district. Chain check is ministry-only.
- Staff only: the public view keeps its prototype examples; officers' names and notes are not public.
