## What I did
- **Adapter** `apps/engine/drishti/cost_form/pswif.py`: the website's form (PSWIF v1.0, the flat JSON of its
  `serialize()`) becomes the engine's cost form and a work record. Only what the agency wrote is filled; the work
  type comes from the title before the scope; BOQ rows become lines (unit aliases, basis, SoR code, GeM id); a line's
  justification confirms that line's confirm checks, the rate-deviation note the whole-estimate ones.
- `cost_form/versions.py`: the fields PSWIF never asks (district code, block, soil, GST split…); the required checks
  skip them instead of asking the agency for fields its form doesn't have.
- **Route** `POST /api/v1/work-form/check` (public view, nothing stored, 400 KB cap): finds the district by the name in
  section C, runs the four agents (`explain.py` trace) and returns the form's fix list.
- **Rate index**: forms are grouped by the agency that filed them (`rates.agency_of`) and dated by the form's own
  filing date (`filed_on`), so the "3+ other agencies, never its own" rule works for website filings.
- **Examples** `data/trial/work_form_examples.py` → `apps/web/public/work-form/examples.json`: 12 filings by made-up
  agencies in made-up villages of the real district of Jaunpur, checked by the real engines against the dashboard's
  40,935 works plus 11 made-up background filings. The script refuses to write unless each example raises exactly
  its questions.
- **Page** `/work-form`: example chips grouped by agent; a chip fills the framed form through the form's own
  `restore()`; the side panel shows the story and each agent's verdict; `?example=<id>` deep links; "Check this form
  with the agents" runs the live route on whatever is in the form. When the form is a loaded example, the panel says
  whether the live answer matches the worked verdict, and if not, why (the database holds real records only).
- The public dashboard has a card under the ten examples linking to the form's filings.
- Engine fixes found by the examples: "complete 0 days after the work could start" (a work recorded complete before
  its own planned start now counts from the sanction); "2,614 bag" → "2,614 bags" (`catalog.quantity_text`).

## Decisions made (and why)
- Examples are a static file, like the showcase and the explain examples: they need no sign-in and nothing on the
  hosted database, and judges see the same thing every time.
- Made-up agencies and villages only, in a real district; no names, phone numbers or MP names in any filing.
  Villages must read as places to the Duplicate place reader ("Belwa", "Kotwa" are read as misspelt words).
- The live check of an example is not forced to match: the page says what differs and why, rather than putting
  made-up filings into a real district's comparison.

## Problems, traps, surprises
- A one-finding work is MEDIUM, not in the review queue (fusion v3's 1-in-4 prior until findings are read by eye);
  only the split reaches the queue on its own. Say "the agent asks a question", not "queued".
- In a live check on the hosted database, whole-estimate Cost can't compare yet: no other agencies' forms are saved
  in any district, and the SoR files wait on T096. Cost then says so (shown, never a pass).
- `pgserver` fails as a background task in this sandbox (permission denied on its own files); run the e2e in the
  foreground.
- `npx prettier --write` reformats files this repo keeps with long lines; don't run it on existing files.
