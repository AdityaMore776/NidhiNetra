## Decisions made (and why)
- **v3 as the default score**: in v2 no single engine could reach High, so 4% of planted problems were queued;
  v3 uses measured hit rates, so a well-read finding queues alone and an unread kind cannot. Rollback kept.
- Splits and repeats stay Medium alone: they read right ~45-52% of the time. Raising them needs officer verdicts
  (T070 hook `Reliability.with_verdicts`), not a cut-off change.
- Office after-sanction habit is a fact, not a finding (R14 already counts the work: no double count).
- New kinds never read by eye (`REPAIR_SOON_AFTER_BUILT`, R06, unit rate) count at the prior 1 in 4: they show,
  but cannot queue a work alone, until someone reads ~20 of them.
